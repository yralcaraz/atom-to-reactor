level 1Pipeline

Each folder of kinetics/ is one stage. From the DFT energies and the MD solvation to the concentrations in each tube, which are compared with the measured spectra.

data/Inputs
  • snapshot, species, medium: DFT + MD solvation
  • network: R1–R9, N10–N15
  • lab_nmr → observables: spectra → shares
→
thermo/G in solution, ΔG_rxn and its standard error σ
→
microkinetics/Barrier rule (Marcus or capped BEP), Eyring, k_f and k_r
→
reactor/Mass-action ODEs along the temperature history of each tube
→
fitting/Predicted against measured shares, χ², best fit, profiles
→
notebooks/ + demo/Figures and tables of the stored fits

scripts/run_fit.py launches the fitting stage and writes its results to notebooks/results/<run>/; the notebooks read them from there. The lab shares (data/observables.py) enter at the fitting stage.

level 2One evaluation of the fit, step by step

What happens each time the optimiser tries a set of barriers θ. This is the most frequently traversed chain: an unexpected χ² originates in one of these steps.

  1. scripts/run_fit.py · stage_stepsone step S1…S5 under one reading (10 min, 1 h or 8 h)
    1. fitting/estimation.py · fit_structureSobol screen of the box, bounded least squares from the best points, final polish
      1. fitting/estimation.py · FitProblem.residuals(θ)
        1. fitting/candidates.py · FitStructure.build(θ)barriers per class → a ModelSpec with its own network
        2. fitting/residuals.py · calculate_residualsloops over the tubes
          1. fitting/residuals.py · calculate_predicted_sharesone tube
            1. fitting/residuals.py · build_sample_historyage of the reading, then the heating episodes between spectra
            2. reactor/protocol.py · simulate_historyintegrates the history segment by segment
              1. microkinetics/models.py · ModelSpec.calculate_rates(T)
                1. microkinetics/rates.py · calculate_rate_constantsbarriers.py: Marcus or capped BEP; Eyring; detailed balance
                  1. thermo/reaction.py · calculate_reaction_thermoΔG_rxn and K_eq from solution.py, gas.py, standard_state.py, species.py, medium.py, snapshot.py
              2. reactor/engine.py · MassActionSystem.integrateBDF with the analytic Jacobian
            3. fitting/residuals.py · _block_shares → _whiten(predicted − measured share) / σ, scaled for the sum constraint
χ² = Σ r² (plus the prior of the freed energies in M3-split)
